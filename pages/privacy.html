Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026. Your privacy matters to us.

1. Information We Collect

When you use BloxCartel, we may collect:

  • Roblox Username — required at checkout for in-game delivery.
  • Email Address — if provided for order confirmations.
  • Payment Information — processed and stored by Stripe. We never see or store full card details.
  • Usage Data — pages visited, browser type, IP address, via Google Analytics 4.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your order.
  • To provide customer support via Discord.
  • To improve our site experience using anonymous analytics.
  • To communicate order updates if you've provided contact info.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing (Stripe Privacy Policy).
  • Google Analytics — anonymous site analytics.

4. Cookies

We use cookies for session management (cart state) and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect site functionality.

5. Data Retention

Order data is retained for up to 90 days for support purposes, then deleted. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. Contact us via Discord and we'll process your request within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

BloxCartel is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via Discord.
