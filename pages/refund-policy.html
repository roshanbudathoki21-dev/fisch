Refund Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026. We stand behind every order.

Our Commitment

BloxCartel is committed to delivering every order as described. If we fail to deliver, you will get your money back — no questions asked.

When You're Eligible for a Refund

  • Your order was not delivered within 24 hours of payment.
  • The wrong items were delivered.
  • You were charged but received no order confirmation.
  • A technical error prevented delivery and we cannot resolve it.

When Refunds Are Not Issued

  • The order was successfully delivered in-game.
  • You provided an incorrect Roblox username and delivery was attempted.
  • You were not online to receive delivery and did not respond within 24 hours.
  • A chargeback was filed without first contacting our support.

How to Request a Refund

  1. Join our Discord: discord.gg/8vHzMDbBay
  2. Open a support ticket and provide your order details.
  3. Our team will review and respond within 1 hour.
  4. Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days via Stripe.

Partial Refunds

In cases where part of an order was delivered, we may issue a partial refund proportional to the undelivered items.

Contact

For any refund requests, reach us directly on Discord. We are online 24/7 and respond fast.
