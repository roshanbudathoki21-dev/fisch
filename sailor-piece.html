What is Sailor Piece — and why do players want Devil Fruits, swords, and rare rerolls?

Sailor Piece is a popular Roblox action RPG inspired by the One Piece anime, developed by Shadowise Devs. Players sail across an open world of seas and islands, defeat bosses, complete dungeons, and grind their way from Level 1 toward the current tracked cap of Level 20,000. The game spans two full seas — Sea 1 and the expanded Sea 2 — each with its own zones, quests, and endgame loot.

Progression revolves around choosing a Race (one of 23 available, from common Fishman to rare SwordBlessed and Galevorn), selecting a combat path — sword, melee spec, or Devil Fruit — and allocating stat points across five categories: Melee, Sword, Fruit, Gun, and Defense. Your race provides permanent passive buffs to damage, luck, health, and movement, making rerolling into a top-tier race one of the most impactful upgrades in the game.

Devil Fruits — The Rarest Power in the Game

Devil Fruits grant unique combat abilities and are among the rarest items in Sailor Piece. The current tier list ranks Dragon, Fiend, and Kitsune as S+ tier, followed by Light, Quake, and Spirit at S tier. Each fruit comes with its own set of moves, skill cooldowns, and playstyle — Dragon being notorious for raw AoE damage while Kitsune excels at mobility and combo potential. To roll a fruit in-game, players visit the fruit dealer NPCs on Sailor Island and spend 15,000 coins or 50 gems per roll, with top-tier fruits having extremely low drop rates.

Because rolling the exact fruit you want can take hundreds of attempts, many players prefer to skip the grind and buy a pre-rolled fruit directly.

Swords — S+ Tier Weapons That Define the Meta

Swords in Sailor Piece directly scale your damage output and combo potential. The current S+ tier swords include Shadow Monarch, Yamato, Dragon Goddess, Escanor, Atomic, Abyssal Empress, and Ice Queen. True Aizen sits just below in S tier and is considered the most accessible endgame sword thanks to its low cooldown and consistent DPS. Swords are obtained by defeating specific bosses, completing late-game dungeons like Soul Society and Judgement Island in Sea 2, or through the game's enchant system (E0–E10). Getting a max-enchant S+ sword can take weeks of consistent boss farming.

Races, Clans, Haki & the Full Progression System

Beyond fruits and swords, Sailor Piece layers in a deep progression system including Haki (Observation, Armament, and Conqueror's), 14 Clans, 25 Traits (ranging from Common 1.10× multipliers to Secret 2.20× multipliers), 11 Runes obtained from the Rune Dungeon, Relics crafted from Relic Parts in Sea 2, and 10 Bloodlines unlocked late-game. Each layer compounds on the last — a top-tier race like SwordBlessed combined with an S+ sword and Conqueror's Haki represents hundreds of hours of grinding unless you have the right gear from the start.
