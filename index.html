Sailor Piece item guide

What Sailor Piece items should you buy first?

If you are not sure what to buy, start with items that save the most grinding time or unlock the biggest power spike. Swords and melee specs are best for direct combat upgrades, rerolls help optimize builds, and crates, keys and shards are best when you need bulk materials fast.

Sun God Sword

A premium sword option for players who want a flashy high-end weapon without waiting on rare drops or trade luck.

Atomic Sword

Good for players looking for an Atomic sword option with simple no-F and with-F tier choices.

Mythical Chest

Useful when you need bulk chest volume for rolls, build progress or stocking up before an update.

Race Reroll

Best when you are chasing a better race setup or testing builds without farming rerolls manually.

Trait Reroll

Use trait rerolls when your weapon is strong but the trait is holding the build back.

Boss Key

A bulk material option for players who want more boss attempts and less time farming access items.

Dungeon Key

Good for players running dungeons often and needing keys in larger quantities.

Shadow Monarch

A popular melee/spec pick for players who want a strong build option delivered manually.

Full Relic Set

Choose single relics or the full set when you want to finish relic progression quickly.
