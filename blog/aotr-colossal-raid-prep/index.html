Updated for May–June 2026: The Colossal Titan Raid made coordination, gear quality, and survivability more important than casual mission farming.

Quick takeaways

  • Upgrade ODM stats evenly so movement, gas, control, and damage do not fall behind.
  • Use potions when a group is ready instead of burning boosts while waiting.
  • Failed raids cost more time than patient prep.

Gear consistency

Raid prep starts with balanced ODM upgrades. Movement, gas, control, durability, and damage all matter because a single weak stat can ruin a run.

Team timing

Use potions when your group is ready, not while waiting in menus. Coordinated boosts create better returns than solo panic attempts.

Role clarity

Bring a build that supports your role. Your team may need burst damage, consistent nape pressure, team utility, or survival more than another greedy damage setup.
