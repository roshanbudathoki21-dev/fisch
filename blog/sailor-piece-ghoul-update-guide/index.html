Updated for May–June 2026: The May Ghoul Update shifted attention toward anime-crossover progression, Ghoul-themed items, and new build routes.

Quick takeaways

  • Prioritize content that improves long-term farming speed.
  • Save rerolls for build-defining race, clan, trait, passive, and Haki outcomes.
  • Use trusted communities because update hype can inflate trade prices.

Start with account stability

If you skipped update week, your first job is not buying the flashiest new item. Stabilize the account first: redeem working codes, confirm your level gates, and identify which new systems you can actually access.

Reroll discipline

The Ghoul Update made rerolls more tempting, but random rerolling is how players burn value. Decide whether your route needs damage, survivability, cooldown comfort, or farming speed before spending premium rolls.

Trading after update hype

New update items usually spike early. If you are trading, compare usefulness against hype and avoid overpaying for pieces that do not improve your build.

BloxCartel tip: If you are not sure what to buy next, choose the item that removes your current grind bottleneck instead of chasing hype.
