AOTR Endgame Shopping Checklist: What Is Worth It?

A June 2026 checklist for AOTR endgame purchases and trading decisions.

Blog / AOTR

Updated for May–June 2026: Endgame shoppers should avoid buying everything at once. The strongest AOTR accounts combine a clean family plan, reliable serum access, useful artifacts, perk support, and enough currency or boosts to keep upgrading after the trade.

Quick takeaways

  • Buy families first if your current passive is holding back every activity.
  • Buy serums first if your group needs shifter pressure for raid progression.
  • Buy artifacts or perk crates when your build is close and needs final optimization.

Buy the bottleneck

The best purchase is the upgrade that unlocks your next goal. For some accounts that is a family. For others, it is a serum, artifact, perk crate, or boost stack.

Keep upgrade resources

Do not spend all value on one item and then have nothing left to upgrade gear or support the build. Endgame accounts need follow-through.

Ask before ordering

If you are unsure, ask support which item gives the biggest upgrade for your account state before placing the order.

BloxCartel tip: If you are not sure what to buy next, choose the item that removes your current grind bottleneck instead of chasing hype.
