Updated for May–June 2026: If you are trying to catch up quickly, the best purchases are the ones that remove grind friction.

Quick takeaways

  • New players should speed leveling and unlock code requirements.
  • Mid-game players should finish core reroll outcomes.
  • Endgame players should chase rare pieces only after damage is stable.

New players

New players should focus on resources that unlock more content. If your level blocks codes or farming routes, progression support is worth more than rare-looking flex items.

Mid-game players

Mid-game accounts usually benefit most from rerolls that complete a race, clan, trait, passive, or Haki setup. This is where small upgrades can make every farming session faster.

Endgame players

Endgame players can chase rare update pieces, but only after their damage setup is stable. Buy the next bottleneck, not the item with the loudest hype.
